Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

FernLearn is a student-built course discovery website operated by Campus Fern. This notice explains how the service handles personal information, including information received when you sign in with Google.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, Google and Supabase authenticate your account. FernLearn uses basic account identity information, including your email address, display name when provided, and the unique account identifier assigned through Supabase. The profile database stores your email, name, Supabase user ID, first verified access timestamp and saved course IDs. Supabase Auth processes sign-in credentials, verification and session information. The sign-in provider may supply basic profile metadata, such as a profile image; FernLearn does not use it as a feature. FernLearn does not request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendars.

How information is used

We use this information to verify accounts, show your personal course library and favourites, maintain account access, provide support and administer the service. The authorized founder can review registration records for service administration. Google user data is used to provide and maintain these account-related features, not for advertising or unrelated purposes.

Sharing and service providers

Google provides Google sign-in, Supabase provides authentication and database services, and GitHub Pages hosts the website. These services process information as needed to operate their respective functions and may process it outside your country. FernLearn does not sell personal information or share Google user data with advertisers. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect the service and its users. External course providers are separate services; their own policies apply when you visit them.

Storage and protection

Account and bookmark records are stored in Supabase. Database row-level security is configured to limit student access to their own profile and restrict administrative operations to authorized founders. The browser stores authentication session information to maintain sign-in. Hosting and authentication providers may process technical logs. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and your choices

Account records and saved courses are kept while your account remains active or as necessary to operate the service. To request access, correction or deletion, email the contact below. We will verify your identity before processing account requests. Deletion requests cover the account and associated profile and bookmarks, subject to applicable legal obligations and provider backup or log retention. You may also revoke FernLearn's Google access from your Google Account's third-party connections; revoking access alone does not delete the FernLearn account.

Eligibility and updates

Use FernLearn only if you are permitted to create an account under applicable laws and sign-in provider terms. We will update this notice if our data practices change.
